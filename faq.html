Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and paying for Studio B.

How do I book Studio B?

Go to the Book Now page, select whether you're booking as an individual/creator or a business, pick an open time on the calendar, then pay using the button that matches your rate. Your session is confirmed once payment is received.

What's the difference between the Individual and Business rate?

Individual/Creator ($100/hr) is for independent podcasters and personal projects. Business ($150/hr) is for company-produced podcasts or sessions billed to a business. You select this honestly when booking — see our pricing page for details.

When do I actually pay?

After picking a time slot on the calendar, you'll be sent to a secure payment page. Your time slot is held for 24 hours — if payment isn't completed within that window, the slot is released back for others to book.

What's your cancellation and refund policy?

Full refund if you cancel at least 48 hours before your session. No refund for cancellations inside 48 hours. To cancel, reach out through our Contact page as early as possible.

What are your hours?

Studio B is available Monday through Friday, 9:00am to 8:00pm.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No — Studio B comes equipped with professional microphones and treated acoustics. If you have specific gear needs, reach out beforehand via our Contact page.
