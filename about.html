About Studio B

Part of AR Podcast Network, built for creators and businesses who want their podcast to sound its best.

Our story

AR Podcast Network started with a simple idea: podcasters and businesses shouldn't need their own studio, or a mess of rented gear, to sound professional. Studio B is our dedicated recording room — set up, treated, and maintained so you can walk in, hit record, and walk out with a clean episode.

Whether you're recording a personal project or producing content for a company, Studio B is built to make the process simple: see the price, pick a time, and get in the room.
