Studio FAQ

Questions about the room.

What's included when I book Studio B?

Full use of the room for your session, professional microphones, and treated acoustics for clean audio — no need to bring your own recording equipment.

Can I bring a guest or co-host?

Yes — the room is set up with seating and microphones for more than one person.

Is the room quiet and private?

Yes. Studio B is a dedicated, treated recording space designed to minimize outside noise and echo during your session.

Do I need to bring my own microphone or equipment?

No — Studio B comes equipped with professional microphones. If you have specific gear needs, reach out beforehand via our Contact page.

Ready to see it in person?

The best way to know if Studio B is right for you is to book a session.
